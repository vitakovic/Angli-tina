Present Simple vs. Continuous

Osm otázek na zápory a otázky. U každé hned uvidíš vysvětlení.

Tahák: jak se to tvoří

Present Simple

zvyk, pravidelnost

  • +She plays tennis.
  • –She doesn't play tennis.
  • ?Does she play tennis?

Po do / does / don't / doesn't je sloveso vždy základní, bez -s.

usually, often, every day, on Sundays

Present Continuous

děj právě teď

  • +He is playing tennis.
  • –He isn't playing tennis.
  • ?Is he playing tennis?

am / is / are + sloveso s -ing. Žádné do / does.

now, right now, at the moment, Look!
